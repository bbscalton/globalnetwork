GlobalNetwork Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what the GlobalNetwork customer app (iPhone and Android) collects, why, and what you can do about it. The app is run by Neuereatec Enterprise ("we", "us"), which provides the GlobalNetwork internet service in Antigua.

What we collect and why

InformationWhy we use it
Account login: your email address, and your sign-in method (email and password, Google, or Apple)To sign you in and match you to your GlobalNetwork customer record.
Your name and phone numberTo identify you as our customer and contact you about your service.
Your village or area, and your location only if you choose to share itTo know where your line is, and to help a technician find you. The app only reads your location when you tap the button to share it.
A photo of your ID and a photo of a proof of address (for example a utility bill)To confirm who you are and where you live before switching on your service.
Chat messages, voice notes, video clips, photos and line-problem reports you sendTo give you support and fix problems with your line.
A device push tokenTo send you notifications such as replies from the desk and service reminders.
Your payment history and service days (recorded by our desk)To show you what you paid, your days left, and any amount due. Payments are made in person; the app does not collect card or bank details.

If voice or video calls with the desk are recorded, the app shows a notice during the call.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites.

Where it is stored

Your account and records are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Firebase Cloud Messaging). Photos, voice notes and video clips are stored in Cloudflare storage. If you sign in with Google or Apple, that provider also processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy. These providers store data on servers that may be outside Antigua and Barbuda.

Only you and authorised GlobalNetwork desk staff can see your account information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your login, profile, ID and proof-of-address photos, chat messages, media and reports. Payment records may be kept as long as needed for accounting and legal requirements. Your connection to the internet line itself is handled separately at the desk.

Deleting your account

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account, or
  • By email to neuereatec@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my GlobalNetwork account".

Your choices

You can turn off location, camera, microphone, photos and notification access at any time in your phone's settings. Some features, such as sending a voice note, won't work without the matching permission. You can ask us for a copy of your information or to correct it by emailing us.

Children

The app is for account holders of the GlobalNetwork internet service and is not intended for children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
